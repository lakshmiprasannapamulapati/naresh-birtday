Happy Birthday My Dearest Love

To My Husband
❧

My dearest Naresh, today the world celebrates the day you were born — but for me, every single day since I met you has been a quiet celebration of the love we share. You are the reason my heart finds peace, the reason my soul feels at home, and the reason I believe in a love that grows deeper with every passing season.

You are not just my husband — you are my safe harbour, my truest friend, my calmest storm, and my most beautiful adventure. In your arms, I found a home I never knew I was searching for.

Thank you for every laugh you have gifted me, every tear you have gently wiped away, every silly quarrel we have laughed about later, and every quiet moment that spoke louder than any words ever could. Thank you for choosing me — again and again — with all my flaws, my moods, and my endless love for you.

If I could, I would give you the moon and every star in the sky. But since I cannot, I give you my whole heart — forever, and always.

Here is to another year of us — of dreams we have not dreamt yet, of memories we have not made yet, and of a love story that grows more beautiful with every chapter.

"You are my today, my tomorrow,
and every lifetime after that."
Your wish is safe with me ✨
